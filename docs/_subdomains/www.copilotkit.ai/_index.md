## Why teams build with CopilotKit

Your agent acts inside the product, learns from real use, keeps every action auditable, and stays open to your stack.

![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/glow-blue.svg)![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/hierarchy.svg)

### Acts inside your product

Ask a question and get back a real chart or form, rendered with your components on the same live screen as your users. The agent can take action through your APIs and MCP tools, or directly in your frontend, and whatever it changes stays changed.

![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/glow-mint.svg)![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/people.svg)

### Learns from how users work

Every interaction becomes a signal. The agent improves as people use it instead of depreciating like static software.

![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/glow-peach.svg)![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/auditable.svg)

### Every action is auditable

Keep a complete record of every action. Your data stays yours, whether you self-host or use Copilot Cloud.

![](https://www.copilotkit.ai/images/home/buyer-case/ag-ui-tile.svg)

### Open through AG-UI

CopilotKit is not a bet on one framework. Agent frameworks and harnesses across the ecosystem already connect through AG-UI.
