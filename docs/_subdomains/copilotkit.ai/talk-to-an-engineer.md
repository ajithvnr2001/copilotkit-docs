## What the 25 minutes actually looks like.
One engineer, your screen, your questions. We keep it short on purpose.
### What happens on the call
  * **First 5 minutes.** You show us what you’re building. We already read your answers, so skip the background.
  * **Next 15.** Architecture, code, debugging. Whatever is blocking you.
  * **Last 5.** What to do next, and who to ping if it breaks.


### Who this is for
  * Engineers evaluating CopilotKit for a real product, not a weekend demo.
  * Teams already building and stuck on a specific problem.
  * Anyone deciding between building agent UI in-house and using ours.


### What to bring
  * The repo, a branch, or a screen you can share.
  * The error, the trace, the thing that isn’t streaming.
  * Your stack: framework, model provider, where state lives.
