# Plans

Start free on Developer and move up when your team or your traffic needs it.

## Overview#

Every organization starts on **Developer** , which is free and includes AG-UI Streams, User Memory, and Automatic Learning, plus a limited trial of Product Analytics. Full Product Analytics is part of Enterprise. Move up when your team or your traffic needs it.

![The CopilotKit pricing page. Developer is free, Pro and Team are paid, and Enterprise is a custom plan for production at scale.](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/images/cloud-hosted/cloud-hosted-plans.png)

### [Start on DeveloperSign in, create a project, and connect your app. No card required.](https://intelligence.copilotkit.ai/)### [View pricingCompare plans and see current prices and usage limits.](https://dashboard.operations.copilotkit.ai/pricing)

What is included in the Developer plan?

Developer includes one seat, AG-UI Streams, User Memory, and Automatic Learning. It also includes a limited trial of Product Analytics. Usage limits apply on the free plan.

When should I move to a paid plan?

Move to **Pro** as you build out your app, **Team** when the whole team needs access, and **Enterprise** when your agents run at scale or must run in your own infrastructure. See [pricing](https://dashboard.operations.copilotkit.ai/pricing) for current plan details.

Is Product Analytics included in Developer?

Developer includes a limited trial of Product Analytics. Full Product Analytics is part of Enterprise.

What does early access mean?

Early access capabilities follow the early-access program and can change before general availability. Compare cloud-hosted and self-hosted deployments on the [architecture page](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/deepagents/intelligence/intelligence-platform).
